01 — Company

A direct line between equipment manufacturers and food processors.

215 General Trading LLC is a Washington, DC trading and consulting firm specializing in industrial food processing equipment. Since 2021, we have sourced machinery from manufacturers in the United States, Germany, Turkey, and China and delivered to clients in the USA, Cameroon, Tanzania, Ethiopia, and India, with new partnerships underway across Africa and Asia.

We work with small artisanal producers scaling up and mid-market plants expanding capacity. For every engagement, we specify equipment against your actual throughput targets, verify build quality with the manufacturer, and manage freight to your installation site, so you buy the right machine once.

02 — Machinery Lines

Production systems we source and deliver.

Each line is specified to your throughput, footprint, and budget, from single machines to complete turnkey installations.

Line 01Milling

Flour & Grain Milling

Commercial pneumatic mills, screening and sifting systems, and grain tempering controls, configured for consistent extraction rates and continuous operation.

Line 02Dairy

Dairy Processing

Modular pasteurization units, homogenizers, CIP sanitization systems, and automated filling and bottling lines built to international sanitary standards.

Line 03Condiments

Condiment & Sauce Processing

Vacuum cooking systems, automated batching and mixing, and precision filling lines for high-viscosity products such as ketchup, mustard, and pastes.

Line 04Beverage

Beverage & Juicing

Cold extraction units, centrifugal clarifiers, fine filtration, and aseptic cold-fill packaging systems that protect shelf life and nutrient quality.

Line 05Edible Oil

Edible Oil Pressing & Refining

Mechanical screw expellers and filtration hardware for cold and hot processing of sunflower, avocado, and other seed and vegetable oils.

03 — Consulting

Engineering advisory, from schematic to startup.

Technical services for processors planning a new line or fixing an existing one.

C-01

Factory Layout Optimization

Workflow modeling that reduces cross-contamination risk, manages foot traffic, and fits machinery footprints to your floor.

C-02

Capacity Scaling Analysis

Equipment specifications matched to your capital budget and target output, eliminating bottlenecks before you buy.

C-03

Sanitary Compliance & HACCP

Pre-audit reviews ensuring food-contact metals, piping, and components meet global hygiene and food safety standards.

Component Sourcing
