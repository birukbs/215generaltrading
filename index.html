Corporate Mandate & Vision

215 General Trading LLC operates as an elite intermediary in industrial agro-processing asset deployment. We serve high-velocity production environments by linking advanced western and eastern engineering hubs directly to high-growth manufacturing zones across the United States, Africa, and the Middle East.

Our core operational competency centers on bridging complex supply chain deficits. We specialize exclusively in configuring, sourcing, and delivering heavy manufacturing machinery optimized for small-to-medium artisanal production setups up to mid-market multi-ton processing plants. We maintain rigorous design paradigms to ensure that every machinery asset we procure minimizes raw material waste, secures strict processing hygiene, and delivers exceptional uptime in demanding operational environments.
